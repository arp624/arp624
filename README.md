diventare un progetto in piena regola.

## Hi, I'm Claudio 👋

Retired electrical engineer, curious about everything: Home Assistant, electronics, homelab,
DIY and flight simulation.

### Where I come from

I spent my working life in the electricity industry, at a large public utility: first in the
**design and construction of hydroelectric power plants**, then in the **operation of thermal
power stations**, with responsibility for procurement.

Big plants, long timescales, and the habit of writing down everything you do — the same things
I still carry with me today, on a much smaller scale, at home.

### What I do now

My "power plant" these days is **MyHome**, the smart home I run with **Home Assistant**.

- automations, dashboards and sensors for lighting, climate, energy, solar and electric cars
- a small **homelab** (Proxmox, MQTT/Zigbee, long-term data, backups following the 3-2-1 rule)
- microcontrollers with **ESPHome** and the occasional DIY electronics project — a field where
  I'm still very much a beginner, and that's perfectly fine
- a **knowledge base** where I document the setup, the decisions I've made and the work done,
  session by session

I'm not a developer: code is a means, not my trade. What I care about is that things work, that
they're safe, and that six months from now I still remember *why* I did them that way.

### Flight simulation

Another long-standing passion. I prepare and publish the **weekly fly-ins** of a virtual flying
club, with which we have just completed a round-the-world trip in 74 legs. To publish them I put
together a small program that takes the flight details, builds the package for the pilots,
updates the website and notifies the mailing lists. It started as a way to get rid of a
repetitive chore and turned into a proper project.
